# The Lighthouse Job

## Scene 1 – The jetty

Fog sits low on the water. A rowing boat bumps against the jetty, and three figures climb out, each carrying more than they should. Somewhere above them the lighthouse lamp turns, slow and unbothered.

**MARA:** Nobody said anything about stairs.

**JUNO (cheerfully):** It's a lighthouse. Stairs are sort of the whole idea.

**OTIS:** How many?

**JUNO:** Two hundred and eleven. I counted on the brochure.

*Otis sets down his crate with great care, then sits on it.*

**OTIS:** I'll guard the boat.

## Scene 2 – The lamp room

The lamp room is warmer than it has any right to be. A kettle is already on. Someone has left three mugs out.

MARA
(quietly)
Juno. Who put the kettle on?

JUNO
Not me. I've been carrying your bag since stair forty.

**OTIS (from far below, shouting):** Is there tea up there?

**MARA:** We are not alone, are we.

The lamp turns once more, and the door at the bottom of the stairs clicks shut.
